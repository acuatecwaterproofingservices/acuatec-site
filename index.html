About Acuatec

From one determined founder to a trusted nationwide team

Acuatec was founded in 2015 with a vision of delivering reliable, high-quality waterproofing. Its owner managed every part of the business herself, from proposals and site inspections to billing and even material deliveries in her own car, which doubled as a mobile office.

Her hands-on approach quickly earned a reputation for quality, and most early clients came through word-of-mouth referrals. Today Acuatec employs about 30 skilled professionals, operates from an office in Mandaluyong City, and is a PCAB-licensed contractor with projects nationwide. Technical consultants provide ongoing training, and the company has been recognized with several awards for outstanding service.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does Acuatec do?

Acuatec Waterproofing Services is a PCAB-licensed contractor offering waterproofing, floor coating and polishing, concrete crack injection repair, rectification and painting works for projects across the Philippines.

Is waterproofing only for new buildings?

No. Waterproofing can be applied during construction or as a repair solution for existing structures.

How do you choose the right waterproofing system?

We carefully inspect the location and site conditions first, then prescribe the method and materials suited to the area to be waterproofed.

Which areas can be waterproofed?

Roof decks, plant boxes, ledges, balconies, exterior and retaining walls, basements and foundations, bathrooms and kitchens, pools, water tanks and cisterns.

Do you work outside Metro Manila?

Yes. Our office is in Mandaluyong City and we handle projects nationwide. Send us your location and we will discuss your site.

Do you offer after-sales service?

Yes. Exceptional after-sales service is one of our core commitments to ensure lasting solutions for our clients.

How do I get a quotation?

Fill in the quotation form on our contact page or call us. We will review your request and arrange a site inspection so the proposal fits your actual site.

Tell us about your project and we will arrange a site inspection and prescribe the right system for the area.
