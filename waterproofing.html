Why it matters

Waterproofing is the foundation of a durable building

Waterproofing is the process of making a structure or surface resistant to water infiltration. It prevents water from penetrating walls, roofs, basements, foundations and other areas prone to moisture.

It protects buildings and infrastructure from water damage, mold growth and structural deterioration, and can be applied during construction or as a repair solution for existing structures, in both residential and commercial settings.

Our systems

Four ways to keep water out

Integral waterproofing

Waterproofing built into the concrete itself with CWS Admix, added to the mix so the concrete you pour is waterproof throughout.

  • Added at the batch plant or on the concrete truck at site
  • Simple fixed dosage of 0.8 kg per cubic meter
  • Complies with the EN 12390-8 standard

Torch-applied membrane

Membrane systems applied with heat for a continuous, bonded waterproofing layer on roof decks, pits, planter areas, tanks and water features.

Cold-applied systems

Liquid-applied waterproofing for walls, basements, tanks, roofs and wet areas, selected to suit the surface and exposure.

  • Cementitious waterproofing
  • Flexible cementitious waterproofing
  • Polyurethane (exposed / non-exposed)
  • Epoxy tank lining (food grade)

Spray-applied waterproofing

CWS100, a liquid calcium silicate hydrate gel sprayed onto concrete. It forms a barrier inside the concrete and seals static cracks up to 2.2 mm.
